Our Shop

A neighborhood shop, the way it used to be.

Wyatt's is an owner-run barbershop in the heart of East Austin. No app-driven assembly line, no upsells — just a sharp cut from someone who knows your name.

Pull up a chair among the vintage décor, catch some good records on the speakers and settle into good conversation. Whether it's a skin fade or a classic taper, every cut gets finished properly with a hot-lather, straight-razor nape shave.
